# Privacy policy: CrawlerData Google Search plugin

This plugin does not store anything itself. When a workflow or agent calls it, the plugin sends the following to the CrawlerData API (`https://api.crawlerdata.com`) and returns the response to Dify:

- the search query or news topic and the country code you configured,
- your CrawlerData API key, to authenticate and bill the request.

CrawlerData is operated by the company named in its legal pages and processes this data to run your account and the API. Requests and their results are kept in your account's job history, which you can see in your CrawlerData dashboard. The full policy, including retention, processors and your rights, is at https://crawlerdata.com/legal/privacy.

The plugin sends no data to any other service. Questions: contact@crawlerdata.com.
